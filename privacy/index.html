Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

Livinai provides AI-powered interior, exterior and 3D home-design tools. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name or username, email address, authentication-provider identifier and profile image where provided. If you use email sign-in, your password is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Your content: room, exterior and floor-plan images; design preferences; custom prompts; generated designs; saved projects; floor-plan geometry; and project titles.
  • Purchases and activity: subscription or coin-product identifiers, transaction status, entitlement state, design counts and rewarded-ad activity.
  • Device and usage information: device identifiers and app interactions that may be processed by advertising, authentication and purchase-service providers. On iOS, Livinai requests permission before allowing tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Support communications: information you include when you contact us.

How we use information

We use this information to create and save designs, operate accounts, provide subscriptions and coin purchases, show and measure advertising, award coins for eligible rewarded ads, maintain security, improve performance, respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

We use service providers to run Livinai, including cloud hosting and storage, AI processing, Apple and Google authentication, RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management, Google AdMob for advertising, and Apple or Google for in-app payment processing. These providers may process information under their own privacy terms and our instructions.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers only as needed to provide the service, meet legal requirements, protect Livinai and its users, or complete a business transfer.

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide Livinai and meet legitimate legal, security and accounting obligations. You can delete your account from the Profile screen in the app. You may also email us to request access, correction or deletion. Some records may be retained where required by law.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted network connections, access controls and hashed passwords. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Livinai is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to us.

Your choices

You can manage photo permissions, tracking permission and other device permissions in iOS Settings. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your App Store account and restore eligible purchases inside Livinai.

Changes

We may update this policy as Livinai changes. We will post the updated policy here and revise the effective date.

Contact

Email livinai2025@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.
