Official support

Livinai Support

Get help with your account, purchases, subscriptions or AI designs.

Contact us

Email livinai2025@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue.

Email support

Purchases and subscriptions

  • If a purchase is missing, open Profile → Upgrade → Restore Purchases.
  • Manage or cancel an active subscription from the Livinai Pro screen or your Apple subscription settings.
  • Apple processes all iOS payments and refund requests.

Account and sign-in

  • Sign in using the same Apple or Google method originally used for the account.
  • To permanently delete your account and Livinai data, open Profile → Account → Delete Account.
  • Deleting your Livinai account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Design or technical issues

  • Use a clear, well-lit image and keep Livinai open while a design is processing.
  • Check your internet connection and install the latest available Livinai version.
  • If the issue continues, email us with the design mode, approximate time, and a screenshot of the error.
For your security

Never send passwords, Apple verification codes, or full payment-card details. Livinai support will not ask for them.
