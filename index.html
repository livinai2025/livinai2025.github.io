FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Our support page has more help.

Visit support
Is Livinai free?

Every new account gets 2 free designs. After that, you can subscribe to Livinai Pro, buy coins, or earn coins by watching a short ad.

Which phones does it work on?

Livinai is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

Do I need an account?

Yes, so your designs are saved. Sign in with Apple or Google. There is no password to remember.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Your photos and designs are private to your account unless you choose to share or export them. Read our privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Account → Delete Account. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel that in your Apple account settings.
