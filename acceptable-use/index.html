Acceptable Use Policy

Livinai is for designing homes and spaces. This policy covers the Livinai app, this website and Livinai for Business.

What Livinai is for

Use Livinai to redesign and stage rooms, exteriors and floor plans, to build furniture catalogues and to share 3D walkthroughs of homes and spaces you have the right to show.

What you must not create or upload

  • Sexual, explicit, nude or sexually suggestive (NSFW) content of any kind
  • Deepfakes, face swaps or any altered image of a real person, and photos of people uploaded to be changed
  • Violent, hateful, harassing or extremist content, or content that promotes self-harm
  • Content involving minors in any harmful or inappropriate way
  • Illegal content, or content that infringes someone else’s copyright, trademark or privacy
  • Misleading listings, such as staged photos passed off as the real, current state of a property
  • Spam, scraping, automated abuse, or attempts to get around our limits, credits or safety checks

How we keep generation safe

Every image request is checked by an automated moderation system before anything is generated. Requests that break this policy are refused and do not use credits. Livinai is not an unfiltered or uncensored tool, and we do not offer settings that turn these checks off.

AI models

Livinai is an independent product built on third-party AI models, including models from OpenAI, used through our own interface and design tools. Livinai is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any AI model provider.

If the policy is broken

We may refuse requests, remove content, and suspend or close accounts that break this policy, without a refund where the law allows. Report misuse to support@livinai.app.
