Official support

How can we help?

Get help with your account, purchases, subscriptions or AI designs.

Contact us

Email support@livinai.app. Include your device model, its system version and a short description of the issue.

Email support

Purchases and subscriptions

  • If a purchase is missing, open Profile → Upgrade → Restore Purchases.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription from the Livinai Pro screen or your Apple subscription settings.
  • Apple processes all iOS payments and refund requests.

Account and sign-in

  • Sign in with the same Apple or Google method you used to create the account.
  • To permanently delete your account and data, open Profile → Account → Delete Account.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Design or technical issues

  • Use a clear, well-lit photo and keep Livinai open while a design is processing.
  • Check your internet connection and install the latest version of Livinai.
  • If it keeps happening, email us the design mode, the approximate time and a screenshot of the error.
For your security

Never send passwords, Apple verification codes or full payment-card details. Livinai support will never ask for them.
