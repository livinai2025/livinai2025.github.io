    Is Catalogue Studio available today?

    Yes. Browse the samples or create two images with your own uploads. Keep going with Livinai Pro or Team.

    How does the Team plan work?

    Each person signs in with Google. Your team shares products, catalogues and image credits. Eligible new teams get a 14-day trial with 10 shared image credits. The trial converts to a paid plan unless cancelled before it ends.

    What uses an image credit?

    One successful product upload, room scene, shopper preview or app design uses one Team image credit. Extra seats add 25 credits each month. Paid balances reset on the first of each month at midnight UTC. Unused credits expire at reset.

    How do I pay, and can I cancel?

    Paddle shows the available payment methods and applicable tax at checkout and provides invoices. The owner can manage billing, add seats or cancel from Team account.

    Can buyers see a 3D walkthrough without the app?

    Yes. With Pro or Team, turn any 3D plan into a public link in 3D tours. Buyers open it in their browser on a phone or computer, and you can embed it on your listing page. Turn the link off any time.

    Do I need 3D models or a studio photo?

    No. Start with one clear photo that shows the entire product. Review the AI-generated scene against the source photo before sharing it.

    How fast is it?

    Image generation typically takes about a minute. Larger 3D plans can take several minutes.

    Can I use the images in listings and ads?

    Yes, the images you make are yours to use. For property listings, mark them as "virtually staged" where local rules ask for it.

    Does it work in my country?

    The Livinai app is available on iOS and Android. App availability and shopping results vary by country.

    Can it connect to our website or systems?

    Yes. Every catalogue you publish gives you one line of code that adds a "See it in your room" button to your product pages. Shopper previews are available with Pro and use shared image credits on Team. Using something else? Tell us and we'll help.

    What happens to our photos?

    Livinai processes your uploads to create and deliver the images you request. See our privacy policy.
