Refund Policy

How to request a refund for a Livinai purchase.

Livinai Team

Creem is our reseller and the merchant of record for Livinai Team purchases. Your statutory consumer rights always apply.

To request a refund, reply to your Creem receipt or email support@livinai.app with the email address you paid with. Approved refunds are issued through Creem to your original payment method.

App Store and Google Play purchases

If you purchased in the Livinai app, request a refund through the store that billed you. Each store handles requests under its own refund policy.

Help with a purchase

For product help, contact support@livinai.app. To stop future Team renewals, open Team account and choose Manage billing and invoices.
